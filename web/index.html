How it works

From a notebook page to clean books in four steps.

1

Snap

Photograph a notebook page or a receipt, or type a quick note: “Sold 3 rice, 250 each. Marie owes 500.”

2

Read on the device

Gemini Nano turns it into sales, costs, credit and payments, right on the device.

3

Check the math

Kaye checks every line and the page total. If the numbers don't add up, it asks you, and only then offers the cloud.

4

Sync and show

Firestore keeps it all offline and syncs when there's signal. The month record is ready to show a lender.

Privacy, in plain words

Your money, your customers, your notebook.

  • Photos stay on the device. One goes to the cloud only after you tap “Send this photo”.
  • The AI never sees customer names in the cloud: they're replaced first and put back on the device.
  • Only you can read your notebook. Firestore security rules lock every page to your account.
  • No advice. Kaye keeps records. It never tells you to borrow, spend or invest.
