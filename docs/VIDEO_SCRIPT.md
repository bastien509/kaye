# Kaye demo video script (target 2:50, hard limit 3:00)

**Format:** phone screen recording (portrait) next to a Chrome screen recording at 1080p, English voice-over, English subtitles. Put Kreyòl lines in quotes.

**Before you record:**
- **[CHECK]** The on-device moment needs Gemini Nano: a Pixel 9/10 or Galaxy S25 (your S24 Ultra reports "feature not available"), or Chrome desktop with the Prompt API and multimodal input on. Download the model first (Settings → Get Gemini Nano).
- Use a real notebook page if you can: one page of a vendor's sales, with a total at the bottom. Keep the sample pages as a backup.
- Start from "Try it now, no account" with the sample notebook loaded. Light mode.

---

### 0:00–0:20 · The problem
**Screen:** close-up of a real handwritten notebook page, then the landing page hero.

**Voice:**
> "In Cap-Haïtien's markets, the vendor's bank is a school notebook. Sales on one page, what the neighbours took on credit on another. Credit gets forgotten, and when a microfinance officer asks 'show me your sales', there's nothing to show. Two billion people work like this."

### 0:20–0:35 · The idea
**Screen:** landing page "Hybrid AI" routes section.

**Voice:**
> "Kaye — 'notebook' in Kreyòl — reads the notebook for her. It's built on Firebase AI Logic: Gemini Nano on the phone first, Gemini in the cloud only when it should, with only what it needs."

### 0:35–1:10 · Snap a page, on the device (key moment)
**Screen:** phone → Snap → Take photo of the notebook page → Read this page. Result: lines + green "The numbers add up" + badge "On this phone".

**Voice:**
> "She snaps the page. Gemini Nano reads it right here on the phone: rice, beans, soap, the credit for Madan Pòl. Then Kaye checks the math — every line and the page total. They add up. Badge: on this phone. Nothing left it."

**Screen:** open "Where the AI ran, and why".

### 1:10–1:35 · When the numbers don't add up
**Screen:** Snap → **Page with a mistake** → Read. Amber banner: "The lines add up to 3,760, but the page total says 3,860." Then the consent dialog.

**Voice:**
> "Here the total is wrong. Kaye doesn't guess: it shows which number is off. If she wants a second opinion, it asks before sending the photo to the cloud — a photo can't have names removed, so it's her choice."

### 1:35–1:55 · Kreyòl quick note, names hidden
**Screen:** Note → "Vann 4 mamit diri 250 chak… Madan Pòl dwe 1200, Jean peye 300" → Turn into lines. Badge "2 names hidden", trace.

**Voice:**
> "She can also just say it, in Kreyòl. Nano isn't validated for Kreyòl yet, so this note goes to the cloud — but first Kaye replaces the customers' names on the phone, and puts them back when the answer returns."

### 1:55–2:15 · No signal
**Screen:** airplane mode on → save lines → clock icon "waiting to sync" → airplane mode off → "Synced".

**Voice:**
> "No signal at the market? Everything still saves. Firestore keeps the notebook on the phone and syncs the moment signal comes back."

### 2:15–2:40 · Who owes me + month record
**Screen:** Owed → Polite reminder (Kreyòl message). Month → Write the record → summary + "From totals only" badge → Print.

**Voice:**
> "Who owes me, with a polite reminder. And at the end of the month, a clean record for the lender — written from totals only, never the entries or names. Kaye keeps records; it never gives financial advice."

### 2:40–2:55 · Built on Firebase + close
**Screen:** Settings (Remote Config values, Gemini Nano status) → landing "Six Firebase products" grid.

**Voice:**
> "AI Logic, Firestore offline, Auth, Remote Config, Analytics and App Check — no backend of our own. Kaye: kaye ou, ki konn kalkile. Your notebook, now it can count."
